Privacy Policy for Knock Durian

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Knock Durian mobile application created by Pakorn Poonsawat as a Free/Ad-supported service. This Application is currently in a Community Beta phase.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and uses certain information to provide and improve its services, including AI-powered ripeness detection and community data contributions.

  • Personal Data: We collect your email address via Firebase Authentication to manage your account and track your contributions to the "Expert Lab."
  • Media Data: The Application requires access to your device's Camera and Microphone solely for analyzing durian ripeness signals.
  • Expert Lab: If you choose to "Contribute," your recorded audio and photos are uploaded to our secure Firebase Storage to help train and improve the AI model.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

3. Data Security

We value your trust. All data transmitted to our servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/SSL protocols. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your personal information, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Data Deletion Rights

Users have the right to request the deletion of their account and all associated data. To request data deletion, please contact us at the email address provided below.

5. Children's Privacy
